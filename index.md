# Watch Align privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026
App: Watch Align (Android, package `com.watchalign.mobile.poc`)
Developer: Watch Align Studio, United Kingdom
Contact: watchalignstudio@gmail.com

## Summary

Watch Align analyses watch photos entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, photos, measurements or results.

## What the app does with your photos

- You choose a photo with Android’s standard file picker. The app reads only the photo you choose, and only when you choose it. It needs no storage, camera or media permission.
- The photo is analysed on your device. It is not uploaded, copied to other storage or saved by the app.
- The result of the most recent check is held in the app’s memory while the app is running, so you can return to it. It is discarded when Android closes the app.

## What the app stores

The app remembers one setting on your device: the watch model you last selected. It is not backed up to the cloud and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What the app does not do

- It has no internet access (it requests no permissions, including the network permission), so it cannot send any data anywhere.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
- It has no accounts and asks for no personal information.

## Feedback you send us

If you choose to send feedback by e-mail or through Google Play, we receive only what you include in that message (and, for e-mail, your address). We use it only to reply and improve the app. Feedback e-mails are deleted within 12 months of the last reply.

## Google Play

Google Play handles the download, purchase and any testing programme under Google’s own privacy policy. We do not receive payment card details.

## Children

Watch Align is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
