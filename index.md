# Watch Align privacy policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026
App: Watch Align (Android, package `com.watchalign.mobile.poc`)
Developer: Watch Align Studio, United Kingdom
Contact: watchalignstudio@gmail.com

## Summary

Watch Align analyses watch photos entirely on your device. Nothing is sent automatically. You can choose to save or share a results image, export or share a Calibration Pack or error report, or send feedback using your own email app, as explained below.

## What the app does with your photos

- You choose a photo with Android’s standard file picker. The app reads only the photo you choose, and only when you choose it. It needs no storage, camera or media permission.
- The photo is analysed on your device. It is not uploaded, copied to other storage or saved by the app.
- Selected photographs and analysis close-up images are processed locally and are not saved by the app. Numerical measurements may be retained as described below.
- You can explicitly save a newly generated results image to your Gallery (Pictures › Watch Align on Android 10 or later), or choose a location through Android's Save as picker (Android 9). The saved image contains the dial view, the result, key marker findings and app branding, not the original photo's embedded metadata.
- You can explicitly share the generated results image through Android's share sheet. A temporary copy is kept in the app's private cache for sharing, retaining up to the five newest copies. Sharing hands the image to the app or service you choose; that service's own privacy policy applies to what happens next.

## What the app stores

Watch Align remembers the watch model you last selected and your calibration settings. In the current Internal testing version, it also stores your active Calibration Pack, any custom packs you create or import, up to five earlier saved revisions of each custom pack, and numerical measurement snapshots from your latest analyses, including a history of up to ten snapshots. These snapshots contain measurements and related analysis information, not watch photographs or close-up images.

These settings, packs and measurement records stay in the app's private storage on your device. Android backup is disabled. You can delete custom packs in the app. Uninstalling Watch Align or clearing its app data removes its private stored data.

Result images you choose to save remain in your Gallery or chosen location until you delete them; they are outside the app's private storage. Temporary result-image copies for sharing remain in the app's private cache until older ones are pruned or the app's data is cleared.

If the app crashes, it may save the latest error report privately on your device. The report contains the app version, Android version, device manufacturer and model, and technical error details. It is not sent automatically. The start screen offers a Share error report button, which opens Android's sharing screen so you can choose whether and where to send it.

When you choose to export or share a Calibration Pack, Android's file or sharing screen lets you choose where it goes. An exported pack includes its name, description and any author text you enter. Do not include information in these fields that you do not want to share. An exported or shared copy is outside the app's private storage and is not removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What the app does not do

- It has no internet access and does not request the network permission. It needs no camera, storage or media permission. The Google Play build requests the Play licence-check permission (com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE). Nothing is sent automatically; saving or sharing a results image, optional exports, sharing and feedback use Android’s system interface or your own email app, and any onward sharing is under your control and the receiving service's privacy policy.
- It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. There is no automatic or remote crash reporting. Optional error reports are stored locally until you choose to share them.
- It has no accounts and asks for no personal information.

## Feedback you send us

Feedback is optional. The app’s feedback button opens your own email app with the app version, Android version and device manufacturer/model prefilled. You can review these details before sending. If you choose to send feedback by e-mail or through Google Play, we receive only what you include in that message (and, for e-mail, your address). We use it only to reply and improve the app. Feedback e-mails are deleted within 12 months of the last reply.

## Google Play

Google Play handles the download, purchase and any testing programme under Google’s own privacy policy. We do not receive payment card details.

## Children

Watch Align is not directed at children.
